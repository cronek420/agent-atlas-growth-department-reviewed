# Master Linear Build Plan

1. **Phase 00 — Decision Lock and Scope Control:** Convert all current answers, recommendations, assumptions, and unresolved items into controlled project decisions before implementation.
2. **Phase 01 — Workspace, Repository, and Project Memory:** Create the standalone repository, canonical memory files, protected boundaries, and repeatable local setup.
3. **Phase 02 — Capability Registry and Skill Foundation:** Inspect, classify, install, or specify the smallest safe skill and connector set required for later phases.
4. **Phase 03 — Governance, Security, Approval, and Autonomy Policies:** Create deterministic rules governing what agents may read, write, publish, send, spend, or escalate.
5. **Phase 04 — Data Contracts and State Model:** Define versioned schemas and deterministic lifecycle states before dependent code is built.
6. **Phase 05 — Google Drive Living-Document Control Center:** Design and safely create the dedicated Drive folder, managed-file registry, Docs/Sheets templates, and synchronization contract.
7. **Phase 06 — Product and Company Onboarding:** Create a validated product knowledge base for Rough Draft Builders Club without inventing missing facts.
8. **Phase 07 — Brand and Creative System:** Create the enforceable brand bible, voice, visual rules, accessibility requirements, and creative asset registry.
9. **Phase 08 — Research and Trend Intelligence Engine:** Build an evidence-based research workflow for trends, competitors, buyer language, and platform changes.
10. **Phase 09 — Audience, Offer, and Buyer Psychology Model:** Transform confirmed product knowledge and research into audience segments, awareness levels, objections, and purchase pathways.
11. **Phase 10 — Platform Strategy and Rollout Selection:** Score candidate platforms and select a maintainable phased rollout based on audience fit, reach, intent, effort, risk, and measurement.
12. **Phase 11 — Social Presence Launch Kits:** Prepare platform-specific names, bios, graphics briefs, settings, launch posts, and human verification checklists.
13. **Phase 12 — Content Strategy and Editorial System:** Create content pillars, funnel mapping, editorial cadence, campaign taxonomy, and reusable brief format.
14. **Phase 13 — Content Production Agents:** Implement controlled producers for posts, images, carousels, blogs, newsletters, case studies, lead magnets, landing pages, product listings, emails, polls, tutorials, and demos.
15. **Phase 14 — Copy Testing and Creative Experiment Design:** Create differentiated variants, heuristic scoring, test plans, evidence thresholds, and inconclusive-result handling.
16. **Phase 15 — Approval Queue and Human Review Workflow:** Implement the Google Sheet approval queue, review packets, deterministic state transitions, and immutable approval history.
17. **Phase 16 — Publishing and Scheduling Adapters:** Build dry-run-first provider adapters with validation, idempotency, rate limits, retries, confirmations, and manual fallbacks.
18. **Phase 17 — Community, Messaging, and Lead Routing:** Build safe comment/DM classification, approved FAQ responses, consent and opt-out handling, lead qualification, and escalation.
19. **Phase 18 — Analytics, Attribution, and Revenue Data:** Create a manual-first analytics model, then read-only adapters for Stripe, website, email, social, and CRM data.
20. **Phase 19 — Weekly Execution Checklist and Executive Reporting:** Automate the Monday operating cycle, carry-forward work, blockers, task ownership, and owner summary.
21. **Phase 20 — Organizational Memory and Learning Loop:** Store winning and failed patterns, source evidence, campaign history, decisions, and expiration dates without rewriting history.
22. **Phase 21 — Alerts, Monitoring, Incident Response, and Recovery:** Implement health checks, alert routing, failure classification, recovery procedures, and postmortems.
23. **Phase 22 — Budget Scaling and Paid Growth Guardrails:** Design—but do not initially activate—reinvestment rules, channel budgets, caps, approvals, and emergency stops.
24. **Phase 23 — End-to-End Testing and Production Readiness Audit:** Test complete workflows from research through publication, measurement, learning, failure, and recovery.
25. **Phase 24 — Controlled Launch, Stabilization, and Handoff:** Launch a narrow approved channel set, monitor four operating cycles, stabilize, and create operator/developer handoff documentation.

## Dependency rule

Each phase depends on the prior phase gate. A phase may be split internally into parallel specialist tasks, but the overall path remains linear.

## Release rule

No production launch occurs before Phase 23 passes and the owner explicitly approves Phase 24.
