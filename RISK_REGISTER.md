## Purpose and scope

This register identifies risks visible at Phase 00 (day zero: no git repository, no code, no external integrations, no accounts created). It is forward-looking and does not assert that any incident, test, or account status has occurred — none has, because nothing has launched. Risks are grounded in:

- The confirmed project context in `README.md` and `MASTER_LINEAR_BUILD_PLAN.md`.
- Owner-confirmed facts from the Phase 00 intake (see "Basis" column).
- General, well-established knowledge about platform policy regimes (e.g., "Meta enforces commerce/business-messaging policies," "WhatsApp requires opt-in for business messaging"). No specific account, clause citation, or enforcement outcome is asserted.

Where a risk's mitigation depends on a file or phase that does not exist yet, this is marked explicitly rather than assumed complete.
